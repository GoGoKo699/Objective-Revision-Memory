The central theorem evaluates the largest low-error input set handled by one
arbitrary raw-read strategy. Ordered-endpoint weighted Hamming balls attain
the same exponent. Memory-rate, worst-input covering, and success-exponent
results are grouped coding consequences. The scalar curve, entropy machinery,
elementary geometric count, and coding conversions are established ingredients.

## Useful next work

Collaboration can start from the complete proof and assessment without
repeating the audit history. A specific prior theorem, proposed reduction,
proof objection, or explanation of limited significance is useful evidence.
Follow such evidence while preserving summary size, raw-read access, the
growing single-archive limit, and error quantifiers. An inaccessible theorem
must remain marked uninspected; search failure is not evidence of originality.

If a checked prior implication supplies the full result, attribute the
theorem and reassess the value of the worked example. If a defect appears,
record the smallest obstruction and correct the affected claims. Independent
specialist feedback can strengthen or reverse the present judgment, but it
is not a substitute for the decision already recorded here.

Finite-length optimality, explicit efficient codes, second-order terms,
the full finite pair-coverage envelope, and error varying with archive size
are optional scientific directions. Pursue one for a concrete question or
obstruction. None is required merely to enlarge this result, and large
simulations do not settle its novelty.

## Manuscript boundary and workflow

The public research record contains proofs, evidence, attribution, and
collaboration information. Manuscript preparation remains the final step and
is currently on hold. No submission or correspondence is initiated by this
roadmap. When writing resumes, lead with the unrestricted extremal theorem
and credit its established ingredients; do not claim general objective-memory
optimality, efficient implementation, or a new coding framework.

Use dedicated branches and pull requests under the
[contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Preserve the license, baseline,
original verification artifacts, and reviewed-commit handoffs. Issue closure
records completion of an agreed task; it is not a correctness or priority
certificate. Reopen the bounded assessment for concrete new evidence rather
than restarting a general search indefinitely.
